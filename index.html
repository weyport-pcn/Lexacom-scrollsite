3Clinical safety

No practice goes live, trial included, until its DCB0160 is signed off.

On a previous rollout the safety case arrived after launch. We are not repeating that.

Two standards apply. Lexacom, as the manufacturer, holds the DCB0129 safety case and hazard log for the product. Each practice, as the organisation deploying it, completes a DCB0160 covering how it is used safely there, signed by the practice CSO.

Rather than five practices writing five safety cases from scratch, we are building a shared PCN core. With their agreement, it starts from Weymouth Bay's completed safety case, keeping what applies to any practice using Lexacom, and is checked against Lexacom's DCB0129 and hazard log when they arrive. Each practice adopts it, adds local detail, and its own CSO signs it off. This keeps the work consistent, reduces the load on CSOs, and leaves accountability where the standard puts it.

4How we chose Lexacom

Over 18 months we evaluated ambient voice technology suppliers against the NHS AVT Supplier Registry, with Health Innovation Wessex involved from the start. Mark shortlisted four: Lexacom, TORTUS (via X-on Health), Tandem (via Accurx) and Heidi Health. The PCN Core and Federation meeting then selected Lexacom as the supplier to proceed with, and we published the evaluation on the national AVID forum.

All five practices have seen at least one demo, and we have now seen Lexacom's latest release (version 26.3).

6PCN services

Lexacom has confirmed PCN staff are covered when delivering services for participating practices. Our care coordinators and frailty team have long, complex phone calls with patients, families and carers, so they could benefit most.

Lexacom's capture audio feature records the computer's sound at the press of a button, so it works with our 3CX phones without integration. Because nothing is integrated, Lexacom does not record which phone systems its customers use, so it cannot point us to another PCN using it with 3CX.

Instead, Lexacom has offered a trial for these teams, with ambient templates built for their roles, and a demo for any staff who missed the earlier ones. Lexacom's own experience outside traditional clinical settings is that results are best when the service defines the outputs it needs before any pilot. That matches our plan: we will first agree with the teams what a good output looks like, and settle whose safety case covers staff working across several practices, before any trial.
